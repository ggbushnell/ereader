# Pokemon aux display on the e-reader: plan 2026-09-12

Founder approved 2026-09-12 (/waves). While Pokemon Red/Blue runs in the
browser player served by the reader, the reader becomes a companion screen
built from the game's own assets. No frame mirror. Information dense. Meant
for short form content and to make the open source e-reader platform pop.

## Data path

- The page (`www/app.js`) reads the emulator's work RAM every 5 s
  (`WB._getWasmConstant('WORK_RAM_LOCATION')`, 8 KB, GB address 0xC000 maps
  to offset 0, 0xD000 to offset 0x1000 on a DMG game, same call the battery
  poll uses) and POSTs the raw bytes to `POST /api/wram` on the reader.
  The page knows nothing about Pokemon.
- Firmware (`src/games.cpp` plus new `pokemon_*` modules) keeps the
  last snapshot, decodes it, and draws one of four views. All game knowledge
  lives in firmware plus the pack file.
- The pack `pokered.pack` is generated by `tools/pokered_pack.py`
  from a pokered checkout (`third_party/pokered`, gitignored) and
  uploaded through the games page like a ROM, stored at
  `/games/aux/pokered.pack`. The repo ships the generator, never the assets.

## Pack container

Magic `EPK1`, u32 LE section count, then count entries of
{char name[16] zero padded, u32 LE offset, u32 LE length}, then raw section
bytes. Section names and their byte layouts are documented in
`docs/pokered-pack-format.md` (written by the generator author). Graphics are
Game Boy 2 bpp tiles (16 bytes per 8x8 tile, GB planar order) so the firmware
blits them straight to the 1 bit panel with a 2x2 dither per shade at 2x
scale (shade 0 white, 1 one dot, 2 checker, 3 black), or threshold at 1x.

## Views (portrait 680x920, 1 bit)

- Home (overworld): in-game town map at top centered with the current cell
  inverted, location name box, then party (icon, name, level, HP bar, status),
  badges as badge tiles, money, dex owned/seen, play time, repel steps.
- Inventory (silk right = next page, silk left = back): bag items with
  quantities, money, coins, PC box count, day care.
- Terrain (silk up toggles): the current map rendered from tileset, blockset
  and block layout with the player marker, cropped to a window around the
  player on big maps; under it wild encounters (grass and water slots, level,
  rate) and hidden plus placed items with coordinates, taken ones struck.
- Battle (automatic from wIsInBattle): enemy front sprite, name, level, HP
  bar, types, catch rate when wild; our active mon with HP, status, four
  moves with PP and effectiveness against the enemy from the type chart.
- Center exits the games server as today.
- Refresh: partial on state change, full on view switch.

Final pixel layout is designed by a separate design pass (Fable agent) and
recorded in `docs/pokemon-aux-display-layout.md`.

## WRAM map (verified against pokered and Data Crystal)

Player name D158 (11, 0x50 ends, Gen 1 text: 0x80+ A..Z, 0xA0+ a..z,
0xF6+ digits), party count D163, species D164, party structs D16B (44 each:
species +0, HP u16 BE +1, status +4, moves +8, PP +29, level +33, max HP +34),
OT names D273, nicknames D2B5 (11 each), dex owned D2F7, seen D30A (19 byte
bitfields), bag count D31D then item/qty pairs, money D347 (3 BCD),
rival D34A, badges D356, player ID D359, cur map D35E, Y D361, X D362,
coins D5A4 (BCD), play time hours DA41 minutes DA43 seconds DA44 (verify
seconds tick at runtime), in battle D057 (0 none 1 wild 2 trainer),
opponent D059, enemy nickname CFDA, enemy battle struct CFE5 (species +0,
HP +1, level +14 in battle_struct, moves +8), battle_struct is 29 bytes (level at +14, max HP +15, PP +25). Pickup
tracking: placed item balls use wToggleableObjectFlags, hidden items use
wObtainedHiddenItemsFlags, box count is wBoxCount DA80; exact offsets and bit
rules in docs/pokered-asset-inventory-2026-09-12.md section 8.

## Work breakdown

1. Research (Sonnet): pokered asset inventory and formats.
2. Plumbing (Opus): page RAM mirror, `/api/wram`, pack container reader,
   2 bpp tile and Gen 1 font renderer on the reader, WRAM decoder.
3. Pack generator (Opus): `tools/pokered_pack.py` and the format doc.
4. Layout design (Fable): `docs/pokemon-aux-display-layout.md`.
5. Views (Opus): home, inventory, terrain, battle from the layout doc.
6. Flash, test on glass with the ROM running on the laptop.
