# E-reader rebuild on ESP32 (decision 2026-08-28)

> Panel update 2026-08-29: the rebuild does not use the 3.97" GDEM0397T81. The
> panel is the Good Display GDEH0576T81 (5.76 inch, 920x680, 1 bit) on a
> DESPI-C02 adapter, the same panel the Muon firmware drives. Every "3.97" and
> "HAT" below is history from the retired RP2040 build; the current wiring,
> geometry and page grids are in README.md and SPEC.md.

## Why
The RP2040 clone build (2026-08-27) died on 2026-08-28. Post-mortem, in order
found:

1. Waveshare pigtail Dupont crimps were resistive: HAT VCC read 2.99 V vs
   3.26 V at the board, HAT PWR 2.93 vs 3.3. Panel rail sagged under refresh
   current, controller wedged until power cycle (RST no effect, wdt count 0).
2. The 470uF Vin input cap had been installed reversed on 08-27 (stripe leg on
   Vin), then lost its GND joint during a diode replacement. With no working
   input cap the clone's LDO dropped out on every refresh burst (Vin 5.03 to
   ~3.2), panel went to noise. Same on USB.
3. A watchdog reboot landing mid-refresh, rail still sagging, made the RP2040
   ROM fall into RPI-RP2 bootloader (flash unreadable at low voltage).
   Reflash recovered it.
4. Final: with the MCU disconnected, HAT BUSY sits at 1.55 V (half rail,
   undefined logic level). The HAT/panel BUSY driver is damaged by the
   brownout cycles. That hardware is retired.

Board-side fixes that were applied and are worth carrying forward: input bulk
cap on the supply node (correct polarity, stripe to GND), 470uF + 100nF right
across HAT VCC/GND, pigtail wires soldered direct (no Dupont crimps), diode
band toward the MCU supply pin.

## Plan
Start from scratch on an ESP32, no RP2040. The panel is the known-good Good
Display GDEH0576T81 (920x680) on a DESPI-C02 adapter, not the 3.97" HAT whose
controller the brownouts destroyed.

What carries over unchanged:
- Book pipeline and formats: `tools/` (pdf2book, txt2book, ocr_pdf.swift,
  extract_figures.swift), MPG1/MPG2 `.pgs` files in `data/books/`, SPEC.md.
- App logic: `src/books.cpp`, `src/ui.cpp`, `src/input.cpp`, menu, dual text
  size, image pages, `.pos` per turn, GxEPD2 + U8g2 rendering.

What must change for ESP32:
- `platformio.ini`: espressif32 platform, an ESP32 board, LittleFS partition
  sized for >=1.5 MB of books (custom partition table or a 4 MB+ module with
  a large SPIFFS/LittleFS slot; 8 MB module preferred).
- `include/pins.h`: new GPIO map (avoid strapping pins for the d-pad; keep
  SPI on the ESP32 hardware SPI pins, HAT PWR on any output).
- `src/main.cpp`: replace `rp2040.wdt_begin/wdt_reset` and
  `watchdog_enable_caused_reboot()` with `esp_task_wdt` + `esp_reset_reason()`;
  keep the GxEPD2 `setBusyCallback` watchdog feed. Serial via USB-CDC or
  UART works normally on ESP32 (RP2040 clone never enumerated).
- `SPI.setSCK/setTX/setRX` calls are RP2040-only; use `SPI.begin(sck, miso,
  mosi, cs)` on ESP32.
- LED handling: drop the GP25 code.

Power: if battery, use a buck-boost or a proper LDO with a real input cap;
the reversed-cap and LDO-dropout lessons above apply to any board. Verify
with a DMM that HAT VCC reads within 0.03 V of the board's 3V3 at idle.

Bring-up checklist on the new build:
1. DESPI-C02 RESE switch on 0.47, FPC seated and latched, VCC on 3V3. There is
   no PWR line on this adapter.
2. Adapter BUSY: 0 idle, ~3.3 during refresh, never mid-rail.
3. Boot render, five page turns, cross an image page, menu header shows no
   wdt count.

Addendum: tried BUSY pin = -1 (GxEPD2 fixed delays) on the damaged HAT to
bypass the bad BUSY line; panel still wedged after a page turn. Controller
itself is damaged, not just BUSY. Workaround reverted; hardware retired.
